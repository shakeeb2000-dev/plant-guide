# Questions to ask your boss

Do **one page at a time**. Don't try to get everything in one sitting — you'll get
better answers asking about one line while you're standing next to it.

Paste his answers back to me in any form (typed, voice notes, photos of his
scribbles) and I'll put them into the app properly.

---

## For every procedure (startup, shutdown, each line)

1. What is this job called on site? What do the operators actually call it?
2. Who does it — which job title? Who signs it off?
3. How long does it take, start to finish?
4. What do you wear for it? (hard hat, gloves, face shield, dust mask...)
5. **What are the steps, in order?** Number them. Don't skip the obvious ones.
6. For each step: how do you know it worked? What do you look at, listen for, or read?
7. For each step: what's the dangerous part? What has hurt someone, or nearly did?
8. What's the one thing people always get wrong on this job?
9. What must NEVER happen? (e.g. "never start a cooker with no odour control")
10. What has to be written down, and on which form?

---

## Extra questions for startup

- What has to be running **before** anything else starts? And why?
- What order do the machines start in?
- How long before you can feed raw material?
- What readings get logged at start-up?

## Extra questions for shutdown

- What has to be run empty, and in what order?
- What sets solid or burns if you leave it? (tallow lines, hot meal in the mill)
- What stays running after everything else stops?
- What gets isolated and locked out overnight?

## Extra questions for each line

- Normal running numbers: temperatures, pressures, motor currents, feed rate.
- What are the product specs you're aiming for?
- Samples: what, how often, where do they go?
- Which bin, tank or label does the product go to?

---

## For every fault / troubleshooting page

1. What's the problem, in the words an operator would use?
2. What do you see or hear first?
3. **What's the first thing you check?** Then the next? Then the next?
4. For each check — if the answer is yes, what do you do? If no, where do you look next?
5. At what point do you stop the plant?
6. At what point do you call the supervisor, maintenance, or the electrician?
7. What must the operator NOT try to fix themselves?
8. What gets held, labelled or quarantined?

## Faults already in the app (confirm or correct these, then add more)

| Area | Fault |
|---|---|
| Cooker | Won't reach or hold temperature |
| Press / Meal | Meal moisture too high, cake too wet |
| Tallow | Dark, cloudy, or high FFA |
| Mill / Conveyors | Keeps tripping |
| Odour | Strong smell or neighbour complaint |
| Boiler / Steam | Steam pressure low across the plant |
| Quality | Metal or foreign object in product |

**Ask him what else goes wrong that isn't on this list.** That's usually where the
real knowledge is — the stuff only he knows.

---

## Also worth collecting

- A simple plant layout sketch, even hand-drawn.
- Photos of each machine, each control panel, each valve that matters.
- Phone numbers: supervisor, maintenance, electrician, boiler contractor, after hours.
- Copies of the blank log sheets and forms.
