# What to ask your boss

Three sheets, in this order. Each one exists twice: as a page inside the app
that you type into, and as a paper copy you can print.

| Sheet | In the app | On paper |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Startup | Menu → Fill in your plant → Startup | `INTERVIEW-1-startup.md` |
| 2. Shutdown | Menu → Fill in your plant → Shutdown | `INTERVIEW-2-shutdown.md` |
| 3. Plant flow | Menu → Fill in your plant → Plant flow | `PLANT-EQUIPMENT.md` |

The sheets in the app are the live ones. What you type is saved on that device
straight away, and the **Send these answers to Kiro** button turns it into text
you can paste back to me.

## How to get good answers

- Ask **"what do you do first?"** then just keep saying **"and then?"** until he
  runs out.
- Write down the obvious stuff too. The obvious stuff is exactly what a new
  operator does not know.
- For every step ask the two magic questions: **"how do you know it worked?"**
  and **"what goes wrong here?"**
- If he would rather just talk, record a voice note while he walks the plant.
  That works fine — send me the recording or your notes from it.

## The fastest trick

Reading my example steps out to him and letting him correct them is much faster
than asking him to write a procedure from scratch. For each step he will say one
of three things:

- **Right** — leave it
- **Wrong** — write down what it should say
- **Not how we do it** — tell me to delete it

## The questions that matter most

If you only get ten minutes with him, ask these:

1. What order do the machines start in, and what happens if you get it wrong?
2. What must be running **before** anything hot starts?
3. What must never be left sitting in a stopped machine?
4. Can any cooker feed any dryer, or are they paired up?
5. Is the contra shear on the raw material side or the water side?
6. Shaker then mill, or mill then shaker?
7. How do the UCO tanks connect?
8. What is the one mistake new operators always make?
9. When do you stop and call someone instead of pushing on?
10. Who do you call, and what is the number?

## Fixing the plant map

Open **Plant Map** in the app. Anything with an orange **?** on it is me filling
a gap. `PLANT-EQUIPMENT.md` lists every guess I made, with a column to mark it
right or wrong.
