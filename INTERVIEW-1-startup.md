# Interview sheet 1 — Plant Startup

Print this. Fill it in with your boss, ideally while walking the plant.
Then send it back to me and I'll put it into the app.

### How to get good answers

- Ask **"what do you do first?"** then just keep saying **"and then?"** until he runs out.
- When he skips something because it's obvious to him, write it down anyway. The obvious
  stuff is exactly what a new operator doesn't know.
- For every step ask the two magic questions: **"how do you know it worked?"** and
  **"what goes wrong here?"**
- If he'd rather just talk, record a voice note while he walks the plant. That works fine.

## Part E — Compare with what's in the app now

Open the Plant Startup page on your phone and read my 13 example steps to him.
For each one he'll say one of three things — just mark it:

- **Right** — leave it
- **Wrong** — write what it should say
- **Not how we do it / doesn't apply** — tell me to delete it

That's often the fastest way, because correcting something is easier than
writing it from scratch.
